# Death Quote

A small Geometry Dash Geode mod that displays a large English message in the center of the screen when you die. The message depends on your progress percentage.

- 0–24%: SKILL ISSUE
- 25–49%: SO CLOSE...
- 50–74%: HALF WAY THERE!
- 75–89%: PAIN.
- 90–99%: ARE YOU KIDDING ME?!

The first version targets Geometry Dash 2.209 on Android and Windows. It still needs a successful Geode build and in-game testing before it can be considered ready to install.
